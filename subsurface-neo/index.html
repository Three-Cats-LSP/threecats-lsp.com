Subsurface Neo

A modern scuba diving logbook, planner and dive-analysis experience built on the mature open-source Subsurface dive engine.

Native apps

Android and Windows provide the complete experience, including supported dive-computer downloads and Bluetooth/USB connectivity.

Cloud sync

Choose Google Drive or Dropbox for private app-folder synchronization. Existing Subsurface Cloud compatibility remains available.

Web

A browser version is in development for dive-log viewing/editing, maps, statistics and planning without an installation.

Development status: Subsurface Neo is under active development. The Android/Windows modernization and cloud synchronization are currently being implemented and tested.
